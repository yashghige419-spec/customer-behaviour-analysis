# Customer Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **1,000 customer records** to understand customer purchasing behaviour, product preferences, subscription patterns, ratings, and purchasing trends.

The project uses **SQL, Python, Pandas, Excel and Power BI** for data cleaning, analysis, KPI calculation and dashboard development.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behaviour
- Identify popular product categories and items
- Compare purchasing behaviour across age groups and gender
- Analyze subscribed vs non-subscribed customers
- Evaluate customer ratings
- Analyze purchase frequency and previous purchases
- Identify trends that can support business decision-making

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Customer Behaviour
Analyzed customer purchases across different products, categories and customer segments.

### Category Analysis
Compared purchasing performance across:
- Clothing
- Footwear
- Accessories
- Outerwear

### Customer Segmentation
Analyzed customer behaviour based on:
- Age groups
- Gender
- Subscription status
- Purchase frequency

### Rating Analysis
Analyzed customer ratings to understand product and customer satisfaction trends.

### Subscription Analysis
Compared purchasing patterns between subscribed and non-subscribed customers.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

Created an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize:

- Customer KPIs
- Purchase amount
- Product categories
- Customer demographics
- Subscription status
- Purchase frequency
- Customer ratings
- Purchasing trends
